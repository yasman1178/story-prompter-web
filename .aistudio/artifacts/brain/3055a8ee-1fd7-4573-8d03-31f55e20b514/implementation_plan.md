# Anime Shorts & Reels Studio — Master Standards V16 Upgrade

A major architectural and interface upgrade to the anime Shorts/Reels creation suite, implementing the V16 production specification: pure MaterialFoundry sound design, interactive timeline scrubbing, drag-and-drop on-canvas layout positioning, multi-ratio rendering (9:16 / 16:9 / 1:1), advanced GPU-accelerated canvas VFX (3D Parallax, Dynamic Relighting, Rune Particles with 0–100% sliders and presets), stylized Manhwa/RPG UI components, and resilient dynamic JSON ingestion for NotebookLM story prompts.

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The following design choices were confirmed and will guide the implementation:
>
> - **Confirmed: Drag & Drop Persistence**: Subtitle and CTA positions dragged on the canvas are saved per-shot into the project data model and directly respected in MP4 video export.
> - **Confirmed: Aspect Ratio Cropping**: Switching between 9:16 (Shorts/TikTok), 16:9 (YouTube), and 1:1 (Square) uses intelligent smart cover with cinematic pan-and-zoom to dynamically fill the frame without distortion.
> - **Confirmed: Default VFX Preset**: Newly imported projects and unassigned shots default to the **Action & Thrill** preset profile (high dynamic relighting, floating rune sparks, and punchy camera motion).

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Transforms text-based manhwa/anime stories into publication-ready vertical and landscape videos. Users can visually tweak timing via a timeline scrubber, drag text/CTA overlays directly on the video canvas, adjust multi-layer VFX (parallax, relighting, rune particles) with fine sliders or instant presets, and synthesize pristine Egyptian Arabic voiceovers with zero Edge-TTS stuttering.
- **Target Audience**: Content creators, manhwa/anime summarizers, and video editors producing high-retention short-form content for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok.
- **Key Value**: Drastically reduces editing time from hours in Premiere/After Effects to minutes in a web browser, ensuring 100% compliance with V16 storytelling, pacing, audio fidelity, and visual polish standards.

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

### Key User Flows

1. **Ingest & Sanitize (V16 JSON)**:
   - User uploads or pastes any JSON file containing any dynamic count of shots generated by NotebookLM.
   - The engine automatically sanitizes `voiceover_egyptian` (purging `[...]`, `--`, double dashes, and forbidden religious/metaphorical terms), converts commas/periods into natural Edge-TTS pacing pauses, and maps `vocal_cue` and `vfx_spatial_layers`.
2. **Interactive Preview & Canvas Manipulation (Video & Hook Studio)**:
   - The video preview canvas features an interactive timeline scrubber bar below the screen with Play/Pause, jump-to-second, and loop controls.
   - Direct on-canvas dragging lets users reposition the Subtitle Box and the CTA Button anywhere on the canvas with live magnetic snap lines and persistent coordinates.
   - Quick Aspect Ratio switcher buttons (9:16 | 16:9 | 1:1) instantly re-render the viewport and export settings using smart cover pan/zoom.
3. **VFX Tuning & One-Click Presets**:
   - Replaces binary on/off checkboxes with responsive 0%–100% sliders for:
     * **3D Parallax Depth**: Multi-layer simulated foreground/background camera drift.
     * **Dynamic Relighting**: Pulsing atmospheric ambient glow with directional highlights.
     * **Rune Particles**: Shimmering arcane manhwa glyphs and energy sparks drifting across the frame.
     * **Motion Intensity / Zoom Speed**: Ken Burns speed and camera pan amplitude.
   - Three one-click preset buttons:
     * **أكشن وحماس (Action & Thrill)**: 85% relighting, 75% rune particles, 80% motion (Default).
     * **غموض وسحر (Dark Mystery)**: 90% parallax depth, 40% relighting, 60% rune glyphs, dark ambient breathing.
     * **سينمائي هادئ (Subtle Cinematic)**: 35% parallax, 25% relighting, 15% particles, slow smooth drift.
4. **Official MaterialFoundry Sound Integration**:
   - Complete elimination of all legacy/custom audio remnants.
   - All sound assignments strictly reference the verified `MaterialFoundry/SoundFxLibrary` catalog with audio synthesis and Web Audio previewing.

### Visual Identity & Theme

- **Aesthetic Direction**: Dark Fantasy Manhwa & Cyber-Rune Studio. Deep obsidian slate background (`#080B10`), glowing cyan accent (`#00E5FF`), luminous emerald highlight (`#10B981`), and dark translucent glass cards.
- **Component Styling**:
  - **Text Overlay (Subtitles)**: Translucent dark slate scroll plate (`rgba(10, 16, 28, 0.82)`) with a subtle 1px cyan/gold border (`rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.4)`), soft outer bloom, crisp Arabic typography (`Cairo`), and generous legibility padding.
  - **CTA Subscription Badge**: Glowing azure neon pill-card with interactive heartbeat pulse, animated thumbs-up and bell icons, and instant visual feedback on click.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Normalized Relative Coordinates (0.0 to 1.0) for Canvas Drag & Drop**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Store `textOverlayPos: { x: number, y: number }` and `ctaPos: { x: number, y: number }` as normalized percentages (0% to 100%) rather than absolute pixels.
  - *Why*: Allows positions to scale seamlessly between canvas preview displays (e.g. 360x640 preview CSS) and 1080x1920 MP4 video rendering, as well as when switching between 9:16, 16:9, and 1:1 ratios.
- **Decision 2: Canvas 2D Procedural Particle & Relighting Engine**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Implement 3D parallax, dynamic relighting gradients, and rune particle emitters directly on the HTML5 2D Canvas context.
  - *Why*: Guarantees 1:1 visual parity between what the user sees in real-time preview and what `mp4-muxer` / `VideoEncoder` draws into exported video frames, with zero extra runtime bundle dependencies.
- **Decision 3: Complete Purge of Legacy Audio Assets**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Remove all old sound references and legacy audio lists; unify all SFX under `MaterialFoundry/SoundFxLibrary`.
  - *Why*: Prevents stale audio mismatches and aligns the app with the V16 prompt reference standards.
